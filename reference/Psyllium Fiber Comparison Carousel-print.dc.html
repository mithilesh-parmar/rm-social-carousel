Spotlight · Psyllium husk
What we manufacture
7g/day

FDA-authorized coronary-heart-disease risk-reduction claim for soluble fiber from psyllium husk.

Gel-forming, water-binding, barely fermented.

Best for cholesterol, blood-sugar, and regularity products that want gel, bulk, structure, and a recognized health claim.

How it compares · Sunfiber / PHGG

The clear, low-thickening prebiotic.

Fermentability: Moderate
Viscosity: Low

Mixes invisibly — tasteless, no grit, no viscosity change in beverages.
Low-FODMAP, gentle, slow-fermenting — well tolerated.

05 / 08 — RM Psyllium
The rest of the field

Inulin/FOS, fenugreek, and acacia.

Inulin / FOS
High ferment.
Strongest prebiotic story, nearly tasteless — but highly fermentable, so gas and bloating are common at higher doses.
Fenugreek fiber
High viscosity
Viscous, like psyllium, with an appetite/metabolic story — but a maple-like odor and legume-allergen labeling to manage.
Acacia (gum arabic)
Low gas
Gentle, low-gas, dissolves clear — but very low viscosity, so no gel benefit and a higher dose is needed.
